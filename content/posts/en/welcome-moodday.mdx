**Moodday** is a personal journal intended for adults in France. It lets you keep your own mood, medication, and well-being records. It does not diagnose or provide treatment recommendations.

## Why Moodday?

Remembering a mood, an event, or an intake precisely several weeks later can be difficult. Moodday brings together the information you choose to record to help you prepare your own summaries and conversations with a professional.

Trends describe only the data available in your account. An observed association does not establish a cause, a diagnosis, or the effect of a treatment.

## Product principles

### Tracking at your own pace

You choose what to record and when. Moodday does not rank users and does not infer a conclusion from a missing day.

### Explicit data controls

The information you record may be sensitive. The product includes:

- HTTPS connections and server-side access controls;
- a primary database hosted in a European region;
- export of data available in the product
  ;
- an account deletion procedure.

Sensitive optional features remain absent from the interface when they are not enabled. When consent is required, the feature must remain disabled until that consent is given.

Details about processing, service providers, retention periods, and rights are available in the privacy policy and public subprocessors list.

## What you can do with Moodday

### Daily mood tracking

In just a few seconds, record how you're feeling. Our intuitive interface allows you to rate your mood and add contextual notes.

### Medication records

You can record a medication, its declared schedule, and the intakes you enter. Indicators rely only on those entries. Moodday never recommends starting, changing, or stopping a treatment.

### Consultation preparation

You can prepare questions, select a period, and generate a personal document. Moodday does not send it automatically to a professional.

### Export and deletion

You can export data available in the product and request account deletion from settings. Complete regulatory requests follow a separate, controlled procedure.

## What Moodday does not do

- no diagnosis;
- no crisis prediction;
- no treatment recommendation;
- no automatic emergency call, message, or sharing;
- no automatic transmission of your data to a professional.

## Availability

The initial service is limited to France and reserved for people aged 18 or over. Optional features are described as available only after they have been enabled for your account and their conditions have been met.

> **Emergency**: Moodday is not an emergency service. In France, call 3114 for suicide prevention and crisis support. For a medical emergency, call 15 or 112.

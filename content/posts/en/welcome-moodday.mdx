We're thrilled to welcome you to **Moodday**, an application designed to help you better understand your mental health on a daily basis.

## Why did we create Moodday?

Mental health is a topic that affects us all. Yet, existing tools are often anxiety-inducing, complex, or lack privacy. We wanted to create something different.

**Moodday was born from a simple observation**: tracking your mood shouldn't be an additional source of stress. Our goal is to offer you a caring, secure, and scientifically-grounded space to understand your emotions.

## Our approach in 3 pillars

### 1. Kindness first

No judgment, no comparison with others. Moodday is your personal space. We use soothing design and positive wording so that every interaction is pleasant.

### 2. Maximum privacy

Your mental health data is among the most sensitive there is. That's why we've made strong choices:

- **HTTPS connections** and server-side access controls
- **European regions prioritized** for the application and primary database
- **No selling of data** to third parties
- **Account export and deletion** available in the application

### 3. Scientific approach

Moodday presents observations based on the data you record. These observations help you prepare conversations and are neither a diagnosis nor medical advice.

## What you can do with Moodday

### Daily mood tracking

In just a few seconds, record how you're feeling. Our intuitive interface allows you to rate your mood and add contextual notes.

### Treatment management

If you're on medication, Moodday helps you not forget anything with smart reminders and adherence tracking.

### Reports for your consultations

Generate clear and comprehensive PDF reports to share with your doctor or therapist. No more trying to remember how you felt 3 weeks ago.

### Caregiver circle

Share certain information with trusted loved ones, while maintaining full control over what's visible.

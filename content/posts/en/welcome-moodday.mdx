Moodday is changing course. The app is becoming a tool for **couples going through premenstrual syndrome (PMS) and premenstrual dysphoric disorder (PMDD)**. The idea in one sentence: she no longer has to explain everything, and her partner finally knows what to do.

## How it works

- **She checks in** in ten seconds: Fine, Fragile, Hard or Very hard, optional symptoms, and one or two needs (quiet, a hug, help with dinner, a hot-water bottle…). Her daily note stays private and is never shared.
- **Her partner answers with a gesture**: a neutral notification, then one tap, such as “I'll handle dinner”. The partner never enters anything about her.
- **The Ally Program**: seven two-minute lessons for the supporting partner.

## Our rules

- She is the only source of her data and chooses what is shared. She can pause or stop sharing at any time.
- We never infer her mood from the calendar.
- No diagnosis: Moodday is a descriptive well-being journal, not a medical device, and never recommends a treatment.
- The essentials stay free, including basic sharing and crisis resources. The Duo subscription (€4.99 per month or €34.99 per year, for two accounts) will open after the beta.

## Your data

The product includes HTTPS connections, server-side access controls, data export and account deletion from settings. Details are in the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy) and the [subprocessors list](/legal/subprocessors).

## Availability

The initial service is limited to France and reserved for people aged 18 or over.

> **Emergency**: Moodday is not an emergency service. In France, call 3114 for suicide prevention, including if you are worried about someone. For a medical emergency, call 15 or 112, or text 114.

_Moodday does not diagnose._
